Setting up a Full Proxy

The built-in iframe proxy only wraps sites. To bypass school/work network filters, you need Ultraviolet — a real service-worker proxy that routes all traffic through your own server.

Step 1: Create a free account at render.com

Step 2: Deploy this repo: titaniumnetwork-org/ultraviolet-app as a Web Service on Render

Step 3: Once deployed, Render gives you a URL like https://your-app.onrender.com

Step 4: Edit index.html in this site and set PROXY_BACKEND to your Render URL
